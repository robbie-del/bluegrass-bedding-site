Bulk Bedding Questions

Buying Bulk Loads of Bedding

How much bedding is on a full semi load?

A full semi of pine pellets is 20 pallets: 1,100 forty-pound bags, about 44,000 lbs. A full truckload of micro or macro flake shavings is 28 pallets at 45 bags per pallet: 1,260 bags.

Do I need a loading dock?

No. Most farms unload right in the yard. The semi backs up to your barn and you pull pallets off with a tractor with pallet forks or a forklift. Let us know your setup and any access limits (tight turns, low clearance, soft ground) when you request a quote.

How do I know what's in the bag?

We carry a signed chain of custody proving the bedding in the bag came straight from a pine tree and contains nothing else. No recycled wood, no binders, no dyes, no additives. Ask and we'll send the documentation with your quote.

Can I mix pellets and shavings on one load?

Yes. Tell us the split you want and we'll build the load.

Where do you deliver?

Full loads ship direct from the manufacturing mills, located on or near the pine lumber sources, to barns across Kentucky, surrounding states, and nationwide. Freight is quoted to your exact address.

How fast can it get here?

Truckload orders typically deliver 5–10 business days after your quote is confirmed.
